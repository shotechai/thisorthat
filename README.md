## This or That — Team Icebreaker
Games release by drshotech

A simple, interactive spinning-wheel game that helps teams connect through light-hearted choices and conversation.

## How it works

- Spin the wheel to select one of 20 numbered questions.
- The selected number stays visible for one second before revealing two choices.
- Share your choice and explain why.
- Click **Spin next question** to continue. Answered questions are removed automatically.

## Features

- Green-themed design that fits the screen without scrolling during gameplay.
- Three spin settings: **Quick**, **Classic** and **Suspense**.
- At least two full revolutions per spin.
- Sound effects with a mute option.
- Editable questions, import/export, undo and restart controls.

## Getting started

Open the HTML file in a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge or Safari. No installation or internet connection is required for the downloaded version.

Use **Full screen** when presenting on a laptop, TV or projector.

## Editing questions

Click **Edit questions** to change individual choices or paste 20 pairs, one per line:

    Tea | Coffee
    Beach | Countryside

Click **Save & start fresh** to apply your changes and reset the game.

Use **Export saved set** to back up your questions and **Import a set** to load them on another device. Browser storage varies, so exporting a backup is recommended.
